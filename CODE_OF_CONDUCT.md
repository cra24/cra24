# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to do their job.

This project is used by people under legal deadlines, often at hours when nobody
is at their best. That is a reason for more patience here, not less.

## Expected

- Give people the benefit of the doubt, particularly across languages. Most
  contributors here are not writing in their first language.
- Critique code, documents and reasoning. Not people.
- When you are wrong, say so and move on. Nobody is keeping score.
- When someone reports a field spec drift or a wrong triage verdict, treat it as
  a gift. They are doing unpaid work that makes the tool correct.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Deliberately misrepresenting what the regulation requires, or what this tool
  does, in order to win an argument or sell something.

That last one is specific to this project. People make consequential decisions
based on what is said in these issues. Confident wrongness about a legal
obligation is not a style difference.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, the issue tracker, pull requests, and anywhere someone
is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to `amr.benabdessalem@gmail.com`. Reports are handled confidentially and you will get
a response within a week.

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or ban accounts. Where a decision
is contested, a maintainer who was not involved reviews it.
